# Category 3 — BRAND STORE: What We Need From You

The Brand Store is your dedicated multi-page storefront on Amazon (a mini-website inside Amazon.ca).
It's **brand-wide**, built once and updated as your range grows — so most of what we need is sent
**once**, not per product.

**Requirement:** a Brand Store needs **Amazon Brand Registry** (your brand must be enrolled/trademark
registered). Please confirm status below before we start.

---

## The Store we build (so you know what the materials feed)
```
Home  →  Shop All  →  Collections (by category)  →  Our Story  →  Recipes (optional)
```
- Home: hero banner, value strip, featured products, heritage band, category tiles
- Collections: one page/section per real product category
- Our Story: your heritage narrative
- Recipes/How to Enjoy: optional serving ideas

---

## A) ACCOUNT / ELIGIBILITY (confirm first)
- [ ] **Brand Registry enrolled?** (Y / N) — if no, we'll guide you through it
- [ ] Registered trademark name exactly as it should display
- [ ] Who has Store admin access / who approves the final Store?

## B) BRAND IDENTITY (send ONCE)
- [ ] **Logo** — vector (SVG/EPS/AI) + transparent PNG, horizontal and stacked versions if available
- [ ] **Brand colours** (hex/Pantone/CMYK)
- [ ] **Brand fonts** (files or names)
- [ ] **Brand guidelines** (if any)
- [ ] **Tagline / positioning line**

## C) HERO & LIFESTYLE IMAGERY (send ONCE, brand-wide)
Big banners need wide, high-res imagery:
- [ ] **1–3 hero/lifestyle shots** — wide format, **min 3000 px wide**, room for text overlay (kitchen, table, rustic Canadian setting)
- [ ] **Heritage/brand imagery** — anything that tells your story (workshop, ingredients, region, founders — only what's real and approved)
- [ ] **Category imagery** — a representative shot per product category

## D) STRUCTURE & COPY (send ONCE)
- [ ] **Your product categories** — how you want products grouped (e.g. Breakfast, Pantry, Gifting). Only categories that match real products.
- [ ] **Full product/ASIN list** — every product to feature, with variants
- [ ] **Bestsellers / hero products** — which 3–4 to feature on the Home page
- [ ] **Our Story copy** — brand-approved heritage narrative (can reuse the A+ story)
- [ ] **Value/trust points** — 3–4 short attributes for the value strip (e.g. Small-batch, Canadian, Quality ingredients) — verified only
- [ ] **Recipes/serving ideas** — if you want the optional Recipes page (send recipes + photos)

## E) BILINGUAL note
- [ ] Do you want the Store in **English + French**? (Y / N) — and will you supply FR copy or want us to draft for review?

---

## What we do NOT need (so you don't over-send)
- Individual product listing photos — those come from the **Images** pack per ASIN and auto-populate product tiles.
- Prices — the Store pulls **live prices** from Amazon automatically. Do not put prices in any Store image.

## Compliance reminders for the Store
- No pricing/promo text baked into Store images (prices change; Amazon shows them live).
- All value/trust claims verified — same rules as images and A+.
- Heritage/"Canadian" language must match what you've confirmed.
