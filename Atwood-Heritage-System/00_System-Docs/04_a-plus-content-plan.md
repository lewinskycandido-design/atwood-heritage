# 04 — A+ Content Module Plan (per ASIN)

Amazon A+ Content (Brand Registry required) for Atwood Heritage. Below is the standard module
stack. Fill copy in `05_A-Plus/a-plus-copy.md` (from `02_Templates/03_a-plus-template.md`).
Images referenced here are separate from the 9 gallery images and follow the same brand system.

**Image sizing note:** A+ module image dimensions vary by module (Amazon provides exact px per
module in the editor). Design at 2x, export sRGB JPEG/PNG. Keep on-image text minimal — A+ text
in image is not indexed and can be flagged; put real copy in the text fields.

---

## Standard module stack (top → bottom)

### Module 1 — Brand Story header (use the dedicated Brand Story module if available)
- Full-width heritage banner: brand mark + one-line positioning + rustic hero.
- Copy: who Atwood Heritage is, in one warm sentence. Facts only.

### Module 2 — Standard Image & Dark Text Overlay (or Image Header with Text)
- Big lifestyle/hero image + headline + short intro paragraph.
- Job: emotional hook + what the product is.

### Module 3 — Standard Three Images & Text (the "3 pillars")
- Three columns: e.g. **Sourcing / Craft / Taste** (or the 3 strongest verified attributes).
- Each: small image + 1 headline + 2–3 lines. Fact-backed.

### Module 4 — Standard Single Image & Sidebar / Ingredient & Quality
- Ingredient or provenance close-up + short story of the key ingredients (from label/site).
- Job: substantiate premium price. No health claims.

### Module 5 — Standard Comparison Chart
- Compare across the Atwood Heritage range (variants/sizes/formats) OR objective attributes.
- Columns = own products only; rows = factual attributes (size, format, key ingredient, dietary).
- Never disparage other brands; never insert unverifiable "better" claims.

### Module 6 — Standard Four Images & Text (usage / occasions)
- Four use-cases or serving ideas (breakfast, snack, gift, recipe). Factual, aspirational.

### Module 7 — Standard Single Image & Specs / Product Facts
- Clean specs block: net quantity, servings, storage, allergens, country of origin.
- Mirrors the label. Bilingual-friendly.

### Module 8 — Brand Trust / Heritage closer
- Full-width: heritage story recap, values, Canadian roots, brand sign-off.
- Optional truthful quality/satisfaction line. No guarantees you can't back.

---

## Copy rules for all modules
- Facts only, sourced. Same claim discipline as images and titles.
- No medical/therapeutic claims; no unverified superlatives or certifications.
- Warm, premium, heritage voice — consistent with the gallery images.
- Consider **bilingual (EN/FR)** where relevant for `.ca` shoppers.
- Keep meaningful copy in text fields (indexed), not baked into images.
