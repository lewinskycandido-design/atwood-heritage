# Category 2 — A+ CONTENT: What We Need From You

A+ Content is the rich, branded section further down each product page (images + text blocks). We
build it **per ASIN** using an 8-part module layout. Much of the raw material overlaps with the
image pack — this doc lists what's **additional or specific to A+**.

---

## What the A+ layout contains (so you know what the materials feed)
1. Brand story header (heritage banner)
2. Hero image + intro paragraph
3. Three pillars (e.g. Sourcing / Craft / Taste)
4. Ingredient & quality close-up
5. Comparison chart across your range
6. Usage / occasions (4 ideas)
7. Product facts block (net qty, servings, storage, allergens, origin)
8. Heritage closer

---

## A) BRAND STORY & COPY (send ONCE, brand-wide — then confirm per ASIN)
- [ ] **Heritage story** — founding, values, what makes Atwood Heritage different (written, brand-approved)
- [ ] **Founding facts to confirm:** year established, family-owned?, region/province, any milestones
- [ ] **Brand positioning line** — one sentence on who you are
- [ ] **Tone/voice notes** — how you want to sound (warm, premium, rustic…)
- [ ] **Approved claims list** and **do-not-say list**

## B) PRODUCT STORY (per ASIN)
- [ ] **The 3 things** you most want shoppers to know about this product (for the "three pillars")
- [ ] **Ingredient/sourcing story** — where key ingredients come from, how it's made (only facts you can stand behind)
- [ ] **Ways to use / enjoy it** — 4 serving ideas, occasions, simple recipes (for the usage module)
- [ ] **What to compare** — should the comparison chart show your range (which products?) or attributes (which ones)?

## C) IMAGERY for A+ (per ASIN)
Can reuse the image-pack photos, plus ideally:
- [ ] **1 strong lifestyle/hero shot** (wide, room to place text)
- [ ] **Ingredient/detail close-ups**
- [ ] **Usage/serving shots** (breakfast, snack, gift, recipe — whatever fits)
- [ ] **Range shot** if the comparison module shows multiple products
> Same standard: min 2000 px, true to the real product. If you don't have these, tell us and we'll plan lifestyle creation.

## D) PRODUCT FACTS for the facts module (per ASIN)
(Usually already covered by the image pack — confirm these are final:)
- [ ] Net quantity, servings, serving size
- [ ] Storage + shelf life
- [ ] Allergen statement
- [ ] Country of origin / confirmed origin wording

## E) BILINGUAL note
- [ ] Do you want A+ copy in **English + French** for Amazon.ca? (Y / N) — if yes, tell us whether you'll supply French copy or want us to draft it for your review.

---

### Compliance reminders for A+
- All copy is fact-based and sourced — no medical claims, no unverified superlatives.
- Comparison charts only compare **your own** products or objective attributes (no competitor bashing).
- Certification claims only if held (with proof + logo).
