## The one rule that governs everything
We only put on your listings what we can **verify from a source you provide** — your product
specs, your physical label, your official website, or your live Amazon.ca listing. This protects
you from Amazon compliance issues and Canadian food-labelling problems.

**That means:** if a claim, number, certification, or heritage fact isn't in the materials you
send, we can't show it. When in doubt, send it (with proof) — or tell us to leave it out.

**We will NOT create, and please do not ask us to add:**
- Medical/health claims ("cures", "treats", "boosts immunity", "detox")
- Unverified superlatives ("#1", "best", "healthiest")
- Nutrition/ingredient claims not supported by your label
- Certification logos (organic, non-GMO, gluten-free, kosher, etc.) unless you hold them and send proof + the logo files
- "Product of Canada" / "Made in Canada" wording unless it meets the CFIA standard you confirm
