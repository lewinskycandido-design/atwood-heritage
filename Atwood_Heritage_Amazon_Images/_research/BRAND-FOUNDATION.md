## Verified heritage facts (usable for Image 8 — with care)
- **1950s** — Noah S. Martin made summer sausage as a hobby while running a machine shop; demand grew.
- **1962** — Noah bought his first smokehouse; hobby became a business. → "roots to 1962" ✓
- **Early 1990s** — granddaughter Rebecca Martin established **N.S. Martin Processing**.
- **2001** — relocated from Hawksville ON to Silver Corners near **Atwood, ON**.
- **2010** — **Jonathan & Marie Ropp** purchased it; kept the same principles and **original recipes**; **family-owned**.
- Renamed **Atwood Heritage Processing Inc.** (to avoid confusion with a similarly named business).
- **"60-year-old recipe"**, "more than half a century", multi-generation. (1962 → 2026 = 64 yrs.)
- Address: **RR2 6687 Line 71, Atwood, ON N0G 1B0** · 519-356-8870 · shop@atwoodheritage.ca
- Mission: best-tasting products; "most authentic provider."
