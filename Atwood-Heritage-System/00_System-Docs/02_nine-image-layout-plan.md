# 02 — Required 9-Image Layout Plan

The fixed job of each gallery slot. Every ASIN uses this exact sequence so the catalog is consistent.
Read alongside `01_image-system-guidelines.md` (technical rules) and `06_claude-to-codex-workflow.md`
(how to generate each one). Codex prompt for each slot: `02_Working/codex-prompts/`.

> Rule: **one job per image.** Fill the spec sheet first; only facts in the spec may appear on an image.

---

### Image 1 — Main Image
- **Job:** Win the click in search. Pure product.
- **Content:** The actual product/pack, front-facing, on pure white (255,255,255), ~85% frame fill.
- **Text/graphics:** NONE (Amazon rule).
- **Source facts needed:** Accurate pack render/photo, correct front-of-pack label.
- **Compliance:** Must match the physical product exactly. No added badges.

### Image 2 — Key Benefit / Product Overview
- **Job:** In one glance, "what is it + why it matters."
- **Content:** Hero product shot + 3–4 short benefit callouts (icon + 1–3 words each).
- **Example callouts (only if verified):** pack size, key format ("Whole Bean / Ground"), a headline attribute ("Small-batch"), dietary flag if certified.
- **Text:** ≤ 6-word headline + short labels.
- **Compliance:** Benefits must be factual product attributes, not health outcomes.

### Image 3 — Ingredient / Source / Quality Story
- **Job:** Justify premium positioning through provenance.
- **Content:** Raw ingredients / source imagery (e.g. beans, grain, maple, berries) laid out rustic-clean; short story line.
- **Text:** "Made with [verified ingredients]" / origin line if documented.
- **Source facts:** Ingredient list from label; sourcing statements from official site/spec only.
- **Compliance:** No "superfood"/health-benefit language. Origin claims must meet CFIA rules.

### Image 4 — Nutrition / Product Facts
- **Job:** Give the fact-driven shopper the numbers.
- **Content:** Clean rendering of key Nutrition Facts + a legible crop or recreation of the panel; ingredients; allergens; net quantity.
- **Text:** Pull directly from the label image. Match units and per-serving basis exactly.
- **Source facts:** Nutrition/label images (mandatory input).
- **Compliance:** Must mirror the actual bilingual (EN/FR) Nutrition Facts table. No rounding changes, no invented values. Show allergen statement.

### Image 5 — Pack Size / Serving / Use Case
- **Job:** Set expectations — how much, how many servings, how to use.
- **Content:** Pack with size dimension callouts, servings-per-pack, and a simple "how to use / prepare" strip (1–3 steps).
- **Text:** Net weight/volume, servings per container, prep steps (all from spec/label).
- **Compliance:** Serving counts must match label. Prep steps factual.

### Image 6 — Lifestyle Image
- **Job:** Help the shopper picture it in their life.
- **Content:** The product in a real, warm setting (kitchen table, morning light, Canadian rustic home). Human hands optional. Product clearly visible and unaltered.
- **Text:** Minimal or none; optional short evocative line.
- **Compliance:** No implied health/medical outcomes. Setting must not misrepresent the product.

### Image 7 — Brand Trust / Heritage Story
- **Job:** Build trust in Atwood Heritage as a brand.
- **Content:** Brand mark, founding/heritage line, values (craft, Canadian roots), any *verified* proof points (est. year, family-owned, region).
- **Text:** Heritage narrative — only facts confirmed by the brand.
- **Compliance:** No fabricated history. "Canadian" claims must be accurate/qualified.

### Image 8 — Comparison / Product Range
- **Job:** Cross-sell the range or clarify variant choice.
- **Content:** Clean line-up of the flavour/size range, OR an attribute comparison grid (this vs. other formats). Consistent product renders.
- **Text:** Variant names, sizes, formats — factual only.
- **Compliance:** No disparaging competitor comparisons; no unverifiable "better than" claims. Compare only own-range or objective attributes.

### Image 9 — Final Conversion / Why Choose Atwood Heritage
- **Job:** Close. Summarize the reasons to buy now.
- **Content:** Product hero + a tight recap of 3–5 verified selling points + brand lockup. Optional satisfaction/quality statement if truthful.
- **Text:** Bullet recap, brand sign-off.
- **Compliance:** Recap only claims already substantiated in slots 1–8.

---

## Sequence rationale
Click (1) → understand (2) → trust the quality (3) → verify facts (4) → practical fit (5) →
emotional fit (6) → trust the brand (7) → choose the right one / see the range (8) → convert (9).

## Set-level checks
- All 9 share fonts, palette, badge style, light direction.
- Each reads at 300 px.
- No claim appears that isn't logged with a source in the QA sheet.
