# Atwood Heritage — Brand Asset Upload Guide

Thank you! Please upload your materials into the matching folders here. Everything you provide
lets us build your Amazon.ca listings; anything not provided cannot appear on a listing.

## The one rule
We only put on your listings what we can **verify** from what you send — your specs, physical
label, official website, or live Amazon.ca listing. If a claim, number, certification, or
heritage fact isn't in these folders, we can't show it. When unsure: send it (with proof), or
tell us to leave it out.

## Where things go
| Folder | What to upload |
|---|---|
| 01 – Logos | Primary/secondary/transparent PNG + vector logo files |
| 02 – Colours & Fonts | Hex/Pantone colours + font files or names (+ brand guidelines PDF) |
| 03 – Product & Packaging Photos | Front/back/sides/top + out-of-pack + range shots (min 2000px) |
| 04 – Lifestyle & Brand Story Photos | Approved lifestyle, facility/production, heritage images |
| 05 – Label, Nutrition & UPC Images | Front label, **bilingual EN/FR Nutrition Facts**, ingredients, allergens, UPC |
| 06 – Certifications & Badges | Certificates you hold + official badge files (proof required) |
| 07 – Icons, Patterns & Textures | Brand icons, patterns, textures (optional) |
| 08 – Claim Documentation & Approvals | Written claim approvals, do-not-say list, heritage facts, source docs |

Each folder has a `_WHAT-GOES-HERE.txt` note repeating this.

## File quality
- Photos: highest resolution available, **min 2000px** on the longest side, originals preferred.
- Labels: straight-on, sharp, fully legible.
- Logos: vector (SVG/EPS/AI) + transparent PNG.
- Please name files clearly, grouped by product, e.g. `MapleGranola_front.jpg`, `MapleGranola_nutrition.jpg`.

## What we do NOT need
- Prices or promotions (Amazon shows live prices).
- Anything you can't stand behind — leave it out and tell us.
