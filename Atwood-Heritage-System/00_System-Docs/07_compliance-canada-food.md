# 07 — Compliance Checklist: Food/Grocery Products in Canada

Atwood Heritage is a food/grocery brand on **Amazon.ca**. Content must respect Canadian food law
(CFIA / Health Canada / Competition Bureau) **and** Amazon policy. This is an internal working
checklist, not legal advice — confirm anything uncertain with the brand and, where needed, a
regulatory professional.

> **Core principle:** claims must be **truthful, not misleading, and substantiated**. If Atwood
> Heritage can't prove it, it doesn't go on the image, listing, A+, or Store.

---

## 1. Bilingual labelling (EN / FR)
- Canadian pre-packaged foods require **English and French** on mandatory label elements.
- When you show the label / Nutrition Facts on Image 4 or A+, show the **bilingual** panel.
- Consider bilingual copy in listing/A+/Store where fields allow.

## 2. Nutrition Facts table (NFt)
- Any nutrition numbers on images/copy must **match the actual NFt exactly** (serving size, units, per-serving values). No rounding changes, no invented values.
- Use the current Canadian NFt format from the real label image. Don't recreate it from memory.
- Don't cherry-pick in a misleading way (e.g. hiding sugar while shouting protein).

## 3. Ingredient list & allergens
- If you list ingredients, mirror the label's ingredient list order.
- **Allergen declaration:** show/state priority allergens per the label ("Contains:" statement). Never omit.
- "Free-from" allergen claims (gluten-free, peanut-free) only if the product is actually manufactured/certified to that standard.

## 4. Claims that need substantiation (or must be avoided)
**Prohibited / high-risk — do NOT use unless fully substantiated & compliant:**
- Medical / therapeutic / disease claims ("cures", "treats", "prevents", "boosts immunity", "detox", "lowers cholesterol") — **never** for a general food listing.
- Unverified superlatives ("best", "#1", "healthiest", "purest").
- Unsupported "clean"/"natural"/"superfood" health implications.

**Allowed only if true and substantiated:**
- **Nutrient content claims** ("source of fibre", "low fat", "high in protein") — must meet the specific CFIA/Health Canada compositional thresholds AND match the NFt. Don't use unless the product qualifies.
- **Compositional claims** ("no artificial colours/flavours/preservatives", "whole grain") — only if the label/spec supports it.
- **Dietary/certification claims** (organic, non-GMO, kosher, halal, gluten-free) — only if the product **holds** the certification; use the correct logo with rights. "Canada Organic" and "biologique" have specific rules.

## 5. Country of origin / "Canadian" claims
- "Product of Canada" vs "Made in Canada" have **different CFIA thresholds** (ingredient/processing origin). Don't use interchangeably.
- General "Canadian heritage"/brand-story language is fine as brand narrative, but must not imply a specific origin claim the product can't meet.
- Only state a founding year / "family-owned" / region if the brand confirms it in writing.

## 6. Net quantity
- Declare net quantity in **metric** (g, kg, mL, L) — required. Imperial may accompany.
- Title, images, and A+ must state the correct net quantity matching the label.

## 7. Imagery honesty
- Product images must represent the **actual product** (no misleading enlargement, no ingredients shown that aren't in it, no serving suggestion implying more than what's included without a disclaimer).
- Main image = real product only, pure white, no added graphics (Amazon rule).

## 8. Amazon.ca policy layer
- Follow Amazon image standards (main image rules, no watermarks/badges on main).
- No promotional text/pricing in images ("Sale", "Free shipping", "Best seller").
- No external links/contact info/URLs on images or in copy.
- No third-party trademarks or competitor references.
- Restricted-products & food-safety policies apply (storage, perishability, expiry handling per Amazon's grocery requirements).

## 9. Storage / handling / expiry
- If perishable or storage-sensitive, state storage conditions (from label) and follow Amazon grocery/expiry requirements.
