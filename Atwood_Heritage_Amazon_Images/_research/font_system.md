# font_system.md — Locked Atwood Heritage Canva-Compatible Type System

Based on the website's classic/traditional heritage personality (serif headings on cream, vintage
badge logo). **Packaging font not yet confirmed** (packaging images pending) — these are the
closest Canva-available equivalents and are LOCKED for the style-lock ASIN; revisit only if the
physical packaging font clearly demands it.

HEADLINE FONT:
Libre Baskerville  (Canva-available)

BODY FONT:
Montserrat  (Canva-available)

ACCENT FONT:
None (two-font system, per §3A preference)

HEADLINE WEIGHTS:
Libre Baskerville Bold for headlines; Regular for short supporting serif lines.

BODY WEIGHTS:
Montserrat SemiBold for labels/numbers; Regular/Medium for descriptors.

CAPITALIZATION RULE:
Headlines Title Case or SMALL-CAPS-STYLE UPPERCASE for short hero words; body sentence case;
icon labels UPPERCASE, short.

LETTER SPACING RULE:
Headlines 0 to +10 (tight, classic). UPPERCASE labels +40 to +80 tracking for legibility.

LINE SPACING RULE:
Headlines 1.05–1.15; body 1.3–1.4.

HEADLINE SIZE RANGE:
~90–150 px on a 2048² canvas (mobile-legible at thumbnail).

BODY SIZE RANGE:
~34–52 px on a 2048² canvas.

ICON LABEL SIZE RANGE:
~28–40 px on a 2048² canvas.

WHY THESE FONTS WERE SELECTED:
Libre Baskerville = a transitional serif with heritage authority, high readability, strong
uppercase — matches Atwood's 1962 traditional-craft, established personality without feeling
cowboy-kitsch or clinical. Montserrat = clean, neutral, mobile-friendly geometric sans that pairs
cleanly with a classic serif for food infographics and stays legible at Amazon thumbnail size.

CANVA AVAILABILITY:
Confirmed — both Libre Baskerville and Montserrat are standard Canva fonts.

ALTERNATIVES (if packaging demands a shift, all Canva-available):
Headline → Playfair Display, Lora, or Cormorant Garamond. Body → Lato, Open Sans, or Inter.

REPRODUCIBILITY (§3A / §30):
No warped/3D/hand-lettered text. Every generated image must state: "Use the locked Atwood Heritage
Canva-compatible font system — Headlines: Libre Baskerville (or closest rendered equivalent);
Body/infographic: Montserrat (or closest rendered equivalent). Do not substitute random fonts."
